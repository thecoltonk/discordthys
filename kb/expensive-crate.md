id: expensive-crate
description: What is the Expensive Crate? Also asked as: crates, expensive crate, $30 crate, are crates pay to win, donation reward.
---
No crate gives a gameplay advantage. The only 'paid' crate is the Expensive Crate ($30), essentially a $30 donation, which only gives a thank-you book with a message from every Cloudsail admin.
