description: How do I buy a rank? My rank didn't show up. Also asked as: how to buy, checkout, rank not delivered, didn't get my rank, store not working, delivery.
---
Add a rank to your cart at store.cloudsail.uk and check out with your exact Minecraft username. Keep "I'm receiving goods for this donation" checked, donate at least the rank's price, and don't donate anonymously or we can't deliver it. Ranks usually arrive within about a minute, but if yours doesn't, open a ticket in our Discord.
